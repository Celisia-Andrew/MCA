Why are schools excluded?

    Each excluded school is counted once, under the first reason that applies, in the order listed. Excluded schools are not estimated or filled in.

    • School with a published FRP percentage
    • FRP published only as “≥90%” — plotted at the 90% line; exact value not published

    Data and definitions

    • X-axis: percent of the school’s total October 1, 2025 enrollment (all grades, PK–12) eligible for free or reduced-price meals (FRP), as published by MDE.
    • Y-axis: students at Meets or Advanced ÷ students with a valid score, across all tested grades. Uses MDE’s published all-grades counts, so each grade is weighted by the number of students tested. Includes both the MCA-IV and the alternate assessment (ALTMCA-IV).
    • Schools are matched by district number, district type, and school number.

    Source: Minnesota Department of Education.
